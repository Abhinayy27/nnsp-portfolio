Deepfake Detection via Adversarial Learning & Feature Fusion

2026.01

deep learning · adversarial robustness

problem —

deepfake classifiers tend to overfit to a single dataset's artifacts and degrade sharply on unseen sources.

 approach —

fused spatial and temporal features via EfficientNetB3 and BiLSTM for video-level classification, then applied FGSM-based adversarial training to improve cross-dataset generalization.

 result —

90% accuracy and 0.97 AUC on FaceForensics++, with improved performance on Celeb-DF after limited fine-tuning.

Medify-AI — Medical Assistant Chatbot

2025.12

llm · rag · information retrieval

problem —

medical PDFs are dense and slow to search manually; people need direct, grounded answers instead of skimming source documents.

 approach —

document-grounded RAG pipeline — HuggingFace embeddings indexed with FAISS for retrieval, Gemma2 via LangChain for generation, Streamlit UI for live upload and chat.

 result —

context-aware answers grounded in uploaded medical documents, with real-time chat and upload in one interface.
pythonlangchainfaisshugging facestreamlitvector db

News Article Classification & Summarization

2025.03

nlp · transformers · summarization

problem —

readers need a fast way to triage news by topic without reading full articles.

 approach —

BERT-based pipeline classifying articles into Business, Sports, Science, and World; paired with BART-based summarization via Hugging Face.

 result —

95% validation accuracy on classification; summarization cut reading time by roughly 60%.
